RIDGEFIELD, Conn. - June 26, 2026 - PRLog -- Target Arm Inc., an autonomous robotics company advancing in-motion drone operations across defense and commercial sectors, today announced a new Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA) with the U.S. Army Combat Capabilities Development Command (DEVCOM) Ground Vehicle Systems Center (GVSC).
